Signs your HubSpot isn't working for your sales team

Most teams don't have a HubSpot problem. They have a process that was never built into HubSpot, and reps who've learned to work around it.

!

Reps don't log activity

Calls, emails and LinkedIn touches live in other tools, so HubSpot shows half the story.

!

Deal stages don't match how you sell

The default pipeline is still in place, and every rep reads the stages differently.

!

Nobody trusts the reports

Forecasts get rebuilt in spreadsheets because the dashboards are missing data or show the wrong data.

!

Duplicate and dirty data

Imports, enrichment tools and form fills have left duplicate contacts, empty fields and companies that aren't linked.

!

Too many disconnected tools

The dialler, data provider and sequencer don't sync properly, so reps copy and paste between tabs.

!

Paying for more than you use

Paid seats sit idle, or you bought Professional for features nobody has switched on.

What we do

Our HubSpot consulting services

01

HubSpot audit and optimisation

A full review of your portal: pipelines, properties, workflows, data quality, integrations, reporting and seat usage. You get a list of fixes ranked by impact, and we can make them for you. This is our HubSpot optimisation consulting service for teams already on HubSpot.

  • Portal and data health check
  • Seat and licence review
  • Prioritised fix list and 90-day roadmap
Typical length: 1 day + write-up
02

HubSpot implementation and onboarding

New to HubSpot? A HubSpot implementation consultant from Stakki sets up your portal around your sales process from day one: pipelines, deal stages, properties, permissions, sequences and dashboards. Then we onboard your team on site.

  • Portal set-up and configuration
  • Sales process mapped into HubSpot
  • On-site onboarding and training
Typical length: 1–3 launch days
03

CRM migration to HubSpot

Moving from Salesforce, Pipedrive or spreadsheets? We map your data, clean it before it moves, rebuild the pipelines and check every record with you before go-live, so reps don't lose deal history.

  • Data mapping and clean-up
  • Pipeline and property rebuild
  • Go-live checks and training
Typical length: 2–6 weeks
04

Sales Hub set-up for outbound teams

Sequences, tasks, calling, meeting links and lead routing set up for SDRs and AEs. This is where our outbound background matters most: we build HubSpot around a cold-calling and multichannel motion that we've run ourselves.

  • Sequences, tasks and call queues
  • SDR-to-AE handover and lead routing
  • Activity targets and rep dashboards
05

HubSpot integrations and sales stack

We track 60+ sales tools in the Stakki directory, so we know which data providers, diallers and LinkedIn tools sync properly with HubSpot and which don't. We pick the right ones, connect them and remove the ones you don't need.

  • Data, dialler and LinkedIn tool integrations

  • Stack cost review
06

Reporting and dashboards

Dashboards for reps, managers and the board, built on data you can trust. We fix the properties and stages underneath first, so the numbers hold up when someone asks how they were calculated.

  • Pipeline, activity and conversion reports
  • Forecasting set-up
  • Leadership dashboards
07

HubSpot training and adoption

A CRM only works if reps use it. We train teams on the HubSpot they actually have, combined with sales coaching, scripts and role-plays, so logging activity becomes part of how they sell.

  • Adoption tracking

Partners

Do you need a HubSpot consulting partner?

HubSpot runs a Solutions Partner Program for agencies that resell and implement its software. A certified partner can be a good choice for complex Marketing Hub or website projects. For a sales team, what matters more is whether your consultant understands how your reps prospect, call, follow up and close.

Whether you need a HubSpot consultancy in the UK or a HubSpot consultant who can travel to your team, we work on site across the UK and remotely with teams in Europe and the US.

FAQs

HubSpot consulting FAQs

What does a HubSpot consultant do?

A HubSpot consultant sets up, audits and improves your HubSpot portal so it matches how your business sells. That covers pipelines, properties, automation, integrations and reporting, plus training your team to use it. At Stakki, the work also covers the sales process and coaching around the CRM.

How much do HubSpot consulting services cost?

Stakki's HubSpot consulting services start with a free consultation. A one-day HubSpot audit starts at £1,200, on-site launch days are £1,200 per day, and ongoing fractional support starts at £2,000 per month. HubSpot's own licence fees are separate.

How long does a HubSpot implementation take?

A focused Sales Hub set-up for a small team can be done in 1–3 launch days. Larger rollouts with data migration and integrations usually take X–Y weeks, depending on how much data you're moving and how many tools need to connect.

Can you migrate us from Salesforce or Pipedrive to HubSpot?

Yes. We map and clean your data, rebuild pipelines and properties in HubSpot, and check records with you before go-live. We'll also tell you honestly if staying on your current CRM makes more sense.

Is Stakki a HubSpot partner?

Answer to match the option chosen in the "Do you need a HubSpot consulting partner?" section.

What is HubSpot optimisation consulting?

HubSpot optimisation consulting is for teams already on HubSpot that aren't getting value from it. We audit the portal, fix data quality, pipelines, workflows and reporting, remove unused seats and tools, and train the team so the improvements last.

Do you only work with HubSpot?

No. We advise on HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive and Microsoft Dynamics, plus the data, dialler and engagement tools around them. That's why we can recommend HubSpot without a sales target attached to it.
