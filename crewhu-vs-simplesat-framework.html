A2 · AI Overview patternBuilt from the sources above

The AI answer currently repeats Simplesat's framing

  • Verdict it gives: "Crewhu for MSPs that want recognition and gamification; Simplesat for teams that mainly want survey feedback across several helpdesks at a lower price."
  • Where it gets that: Simplesat's own comparison page and customer stories, because there's nothing from Crewhu to cite.
  • Wrong price data in circulation: G2 and other directories list Crewhu "starting at $99" or "$99 per user per month". Crewhu's public pricing is $119/mo for 5 users (Listen). The AI picks up whichever number it finds.
  • Ratings it quotes: G2 gives Crewhu 4.7 (94 reviews) and Simplesat 4.8 (166). Capterra, which isn't being quoted, gives Crewhu 4.9 (98) and Simplesat 4.8 (61).

So what: a fair, well-sourced Crewhu page with a quotable answer box, dated pricing and Capterra ratings gives the AI a second source to cite, and an MSP-specific verdict to repeat.

A5 · GapsRanked by impact

Gaps in the current results that Crewhu can take

HighNo Crewhu-owned comparison

The only head-to-head is Simplesat's. Brand searchers for "crewhu" + "simplesat" read the competitor's version of Crewhu.

Use: publish this page, and add "crewhu alternative" as a defensive keyword.

HighNobody prices it at MSP volume

Simplesat charges by responses (Starter = 300/mo). A 12-tech MSP closing ~1,500 tickets at 25% response produces ~375 responses a month, which is over Starter. No ranking page does this math.

Use: scenario table at 3 MSP sizes, plus a "what's counted" explainer.

HighWrong Crewhu price in directories

"$99 per user/month" appears on G2, Spotsaas and others. The AI repeats it.

Use: a dated pricing table on the page, plus ask Crewhu to correct its G2/Capterra listings.

High"What happens after the survey" is unexplained

Simplesat stops at reporting and testimonials. Nobody shows the loop from a 5-star survey to a badge, points and a Google review.

Use: the loop diagram plus GO2 Tech numbers (18 → 130 reviews).

MediumResponse-rate proof is one-sided

Simplesat publishes 31–36% response rates for MSPs. Crewhu publishes "survey conversion" (24%, +17%) but no response-rate benchmark.

Use: get Crewhu's median response rate across MSP customers and publish it with method.

MediumGeneralist vs specialist isn't stated

Simplesat sells to e-commerce, SaaS and hospitality too. MSP buyers aren't told which tool is built around PSA tickets, technicians and MSP KPIs.

Use: core-difference section titled around "built for MSPs".

MediumNo "use both" option

Simplesat customers who want recognition have no published path. Crewhu offers Survey Import and an Open API.

Use: a "Switch or combine" section, if Crewhu confirms Simplesat data can feed recognition.

VerifyGoogle review generation

No source shows Simplesat sending happy clients to Google reviews. If that holds, it's a clear row in Crewhu's favour.

Use: one table row and one H3, worded "not listed" until confirmed.

A6 · Honest strengthsBoth columns go on the page in some form

Where each product wins

Crewhu's advantages

  • Built only for MSPs; every feature assumes PSA tickets and technicians.
  • Feedback triggers recognition, points, contests and rewards automatically. Simplesat has no equivalent.
  • Gamifies metrics beyond CSAT (PSA, IT Glue, VoIP).
  • Google review generation with published results (GO2 Tech +600%).
  • Unlimited 1-click feedback on every plan; no response caps.
  • Higher Capterra rating (4.9 vs 4.8) with more reviews.
  • Implementation consultant on every plan; 60-day money-back on annual.

Simplesat's advantages (concede these)

  • Cheaper entry for surveys only; unlimited agents.
  • CES and 5-star surveys; multi-question logic, custom CSS, multi-brand, SSO.
  • Wider helpdesk coverage outside the MSP stack (Zendesk, Gorgias, Salesforce).
  • More G2 reviews (166 vs 94); strong support reputation.
  • Topic and sentiment tagging on comments.
  • Published MSP response rates of 31–36%.
A7 · StrategyHow this page differs from Crewhu vs SmileBack

Page strategy

  • Archetype: like-for-like on surveys (A) plus scope (B). Unlike SmileBack, there's no ownership/independence section: both companies are independent.
  • Main frame: "MSP-specific platform vs. survey tool for any support team". It's true, it's MSP-relevant, and Simplesat can't claim it.
  • Main battleground: pricing model. Per response (Simplesat) vs per employee (Crewhu), modelled at real MSP sizes. This is where the competitor page is weakest.
  • New sections vs SmileBack: Response rates (Block 06) and Switch or combine (Block 10).
  • Tone: concede Simplesat's survey depth and price for surveys only. Fairness is what gets the page cited over Simplesat's own.
  • Defend the brand: target "crewhu alternative" and "crewhu pricing" secondarily, so Crewhu's own page competes on its brand terms.
Part B · Page wireframe
Be fair, cite everything

Every claim about Simplesat links to its own page or a third-party source. Write "not offered", never "lacks" or "fails".

Date the facts

Simplesat changed its plans in 2026. Show "Last verified: [date]" and recheck pricing every quarter.

Write for quoting

Open each H2 with a 1–2 sentence direct answer. AI Overviews and featured snippets lift these lines.

Voice

Plain, confident, MSP-insider. Keep "BADASS" out of this page: buyers comparing vendors want a neutral tone.

Length

2,000–2,800 words in total. The pricing scenarios and tables carry the detail.

Names

Write "Simplesat" (not "SimpleSat") as the company does. No competitor logos without legal sign-off.

The core difference: built for MSPs vs. built for every support team

Writer notes

Opening line
"Simplesat is designed to measure satisfaction for any support team, from e-commerce to SaaS. Crewhu is designed for one: the MSP service desk, where the person who closed the ticket is the person you want to motivate."
Cover
  • Both send one-click surveys when a PSA ticket closes and write the result back. Say so plainly.
  • Simplesat then gives you dashboards, tags and testimonials.
  • Crewhu also routes the response to the technician as recognition, points and contest progress, and to the client as a Google review request.
  • Why it matters for MSPs: technician turnover and morale drive CSAT; Simplesat measures it, Crewhu acts on it.
Proof point
XL.net: CSAT moved from an estimated "low 80s" to 95% with +20% engagement after combining feedback with recognition.
