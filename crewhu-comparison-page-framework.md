# Crewhu Comparison Pages: Framework + "Crewhu vs. SmileBack" Outline

**Prepared:** 26 Oct 2026
**Scope:** How to structure Crewhu's "vs" pages, with the full outline for **Crewhu vs. SmileBack** and how the outline changes for Simplesat, built-in PSA surveys, HappySat and service providers.

> **Research limits.** Google, Bing, smileback.com, simplesat.io, G2 and Capterra are all blocked from the environment I worked in, so I couldn't load the live results page or the AI Overview (AIO) itself. Instead I ran the searches an AIO answers ("crewhu vs smileback", "is Crewhu better than SmileBack", "smileback alternatives", "native vs third-party CSAT") through a search tool, and analyzed the pages that rank and how the answers were put together. **Before writing, take a screenshot of the live Google results and AIO for `crewhu vs smileback` and `smileback alternative`**, and check §1 against it.

### 1.2 How the AI answer is put together (from AIO-style synthesis of those sources)
For "Crewhu vs SmileBack" and "is Crewhu better than SmileBack", the answer took the same shape every time:
1. **A one-line framing of scope:** "SmileBack is narrower and focused on CSAT. Crewhu is broader and adds employee recognition and engagement."
2. **Focus or positioning of each product**, quoted from its own description ("the only customer feedback system… designed for MSPs").
3. **Pricing**, flagged as inconsistent across sources.
4. **Ratings**, with review counts (both 4.9: Crewhu from 98 reviews, SmileBack from 35).
5. **Review themes**, such as "reviewers switched from SmileBack for employee engagement".
6. **A verdict by use case:** "If you mainly want CSAT/NPS → SmileBack. If you also want recognition and rewards → Crewhu."
7. **A caveat:** vendor claims are promotional, so check pricing directly.

**What this means for the page:** the AIO already frames the decision as **scope (point tool vs. platform)** and gives a **verdict by use case**. The page should provide those parts in a form that's easy to quote: a scope sentence, a dated pricing table, rating counts, sourced review quotes, and a "choose SmileBack if / choose Crewhu if" block. Crewhu can't remove the "SmileBack is simpler" verdict, but it can own the "if you also want…" half and make it the stronger one.

## 2. The framework: a fixed core plus modules by archetype

Comparison pages fail when one template is forced onto every opponent. Use a **fixed core** (what every AIO answer and every buyer needs) and choose **modules** based on *what kind of alternative* the opponent is.

### 2.1 Fixed core (every comparison page)
1. **Answer-first summary (40–60 words).** One quotable paragraph: what each option is and who should pick which.
2. **"At a glance" table.** 6–10 rows, dated ("Last verified: Oct 2026"), with sources linked.
3. **Verdict by use case:** "Choose X if… / Choose Crewhu if…". It has to be honest to get cited and believed.
4. **Proof:** a customer who switched or decided, with numbers.
5. **FAQ:** written from People Also Ask and AIO follow-up questions, with `FAQPage` content (even though Google rarely shows FAQ rich results for SaaS now, the Q&A format still feeds AIO).
6. **CTA matched to the stage** (demo, migration call, ROI calculator).
7. **Trust layer:** "Last updated" date, author with MSP credentials, a sources list, and a fair-comparison note.

### 2.2 Archetypes and the modules each needs

| Archetype | Example opponents | The buyer's real question | Core frame | Modules to add | Leave out |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Like-for-like software** | Simplesat, HappySat | "Which CSAT tool is better?" | Depth and quality on the same job | Deep feature table; survey types (CSAT/NPS/CES); response rates; support and onboarding; pricing by tier; migration | Scope arguments (they're the same scope) |
| **B. Point tool vs. platform** | **SmileBack**, Bonusly, Kudos | "Do I need more than surveys?" | **Scope**: one job vs. a closed loop | Point-tool-stack vs. platform diagram; total stack cost; "what happens after the survey"; when the point tool is enough | A 40-row feature checkbox war |
| **C. Owned by a PSA vendor** (overlay on A/B) | SmileBack (ConnectWise) | "Will this lock me in or work with my PSA?" | Independence and multi-PSA support | PSA compatibility matrix; roadmap and ownership facts; data portability | Speculation about the owner's plans |
| **D. Built-in / do nothing** | Native ConnectWise, Autotask or Halo surveys | "Is the free one good enough?" | Capability ladder: basic → program | "What native covers / where it stops" ladder; cost of low response rates; ROI; when native is enough | Vendor-vs-vendor framing (there's no rival brand to beat) |
| **E. Generic survey tool** | SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Qualtrics | "Can't I use the tool we already have?" | Fit for MSPs | Ticket-triggered vs. manual surveys; PSA write-back; technician attribution | Enterprise feature breadth |
| **F. Service / agency** | Outsourced CX programs, MSP marketing agencies selling review generation, consultants | "Software, or pay someone to do it?" | Build vs. buy vs. outsource | Process comparison (who does what each week); time to value; ownership of data and relationships; cost over 12 months; a hybrid option | Feature tables entirely. Use a **deliverables and responsibilities** table instead. |

**Rule:** pick **one** primary archetype for the H2 structure, and add overlays (like C) as single sections. SmileBack = **B + C**.

### Page setup
| Item | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| URL | `/compare/crewhu-vs-smileback` (set up `/compare/` as the hub for all vs pages) |
| Title tag | `Crewhu vs SmileBack (ConnectWise Customer Feedback): 2026 Comparison` (shorten the brand in the parentheses if over 60 characters in the SERP) |
| Meta description | `SmileBack measures CSAT. Crewhu measures it and turns it into recognition, rewards and reviews. Compare features, pricing and fit for MSPs.` |
| H1 | `Crewhu vs. SmileBack: CSAT Survey Tool or Full Feedback-to-Recognition Platform?` |
| Primary keywords | crewhu vs smileback, smileback vs crewhu, smileback alternative, connectwise customer feedback alternative |
| Secondary keywords | smileback pricing, smileback review, msp csat software, csat and employee recognition for msps |

| Length | About 1,800–2,500 words. Fewer if the tables carry the load. |

### Section-by-section outline

**0. Above the fold**
- H1 as above, plus a one-line subhead: "Both are built for MSPs. Here's where they differ, and when SmileBack is the better pick."
- **Answer box (target for AIO):**
  > *SmileBack, now ConnectWise Customer Feedback, is a CSAT and NPS survey tool for MSP help desks. Crewhu includes the same one-click ticket surveys and adds employee recognition, gamified KPIs, rewards and review generation, so client feedback turns into technician motivation. Pick SmileBack for surveys only; pick Crewhu if you want feedback to drive culture and retention.*
- Meta line: "Last verified 26 Oct 2026 · Written by [Name, MSP background] · Sources below"
- CTAs: "See Crewhu with your PSA data" (demo) and "Switching from SmileBack? Talk migration" (secondary)

**1. H2 – Crewhu vs. SmileBack at a glance**
Table (keep each cell under about 12 words; this is what an AIO will quote):

| | Crewhu | SmileBack (ConnectWise Customer Feedback) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Feedback + recognition + gamification + rewards platform for MSPs | CSAT/NPS survey tool for MSPs and help desks |
| Ownership | Independent (Crewhu, LLC) | Owned by ConnectWise since Dec 2021 |
| Surveys | One-click CSAT, NPS, custom surveys | One-click CSAT, NPS |
| PSA support | ConnectWise, Autotask, Kaseya BMS, HaloPSA, Syncro + more | ConnectWise, Autotask, Kaseya BMS *(confirm Halo)* |
| Employee recognition | Peer, manager and core-value badges | ✗ |
| Gamification / KPI contests | ✓ (CSAT plus PSA, documentation, VoIP metrics) | ✗ |
| Rewards store | ✓ Crewhu Bucks + store | ✗ |
| Reviews / website widget | ✓ widget, live testimonial feed, review requests | ✓ widget, Google Reviews |
| Pricing basis | Per active employee; from $119/mo (5 users) | Per ticket-handling agent; from $99/mo (1–5 agents) |
| Ratings | Capterra 4.9 (98 reviews) | Capterra 4.9 (35); G2 4.8 (34) |
| Best for | MSPs that want CSAT to drive culture and retention | MSPs that want simple CSAT only |

Footnote: "Pricing and ratings checked 26 Oct 2026 from vendor pages and Capterra/G2. Tell us if something has changed."

**2. H2 – The core difference: measuring satisfaction vs. acting on it**
- Explain the scope gap in plain language: SmileBack stops at the score; Crewhu sends the score to the technician, the leaderboard and the reward.
- **Diagram:** *Ticket closed → survey → score* (both) → *badge + Crewhu Bucks → leaderboard → contest → review request → website widget* (Crewhu only).
- One short paragraph on why it matters, using a Crewhu proof point (XL.net +20% engagement, or Succurri's quote that ownership now decides "based on measured facts").

**3. H2 – Where SmileBack is the better choice** *(credibility section, keep it honest)*
- You only need CSAT/NPS and have no budget or appetite for a recognition program.
- Very small team: the per-agent pricing is cheaper for CSAT alone.
- You're all-in on ConnectWise and want one vendor relationship.
- Reviewers rate it as very easy to use.

> This section is what makes the page credible enough to be cited. Pages that admit where the competitor wins convert better (TripleDart, 2026) and look less like self-promotion to an AIO.

**4. H2 – Where Crewhu goes further**
Use H3s, each with one sentence, one screenshot and one proof point:
- H3 Recognition tied to real client feedback (auto badges on positive CSAT)
- H3 Gamify the metrics that matter, not just CSAT (PSA, IT Glue, VoIP KPIs)
- H3 Rewards your team actually redeems (Crewhu Bucks, store, budgets)
- H3 Reputation: from happy survey to Google review (GO2 Tech 18 → 130 reviews)
- H3 Implementation with a consultant on every plan

**5. H2 – PSA compatibility and vendor independence** *(Archetype C overlay)*
- A matrix of PSA × product (✓ / ✗ / partial) with sources.
- A neutral, factual line: "SmileBack has been part of ConnectWise since 2021. Crewhu is independent and works the same way across ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo, Kaseya BMS and Syncro." **No speculation** about ConnectWise's plans.
- Data ownership and export: what happens to survey history if you switch (check with the client).

**6. H2 – Pricing: what you'd actually pay**
- Two pricing tables with each vendor's real basis (agents vs. active employees), dated.
- **Scenario block (the key argument):** "A 20-person MSP with 12 techs"
  - SmileBack for CSAT + a separate recognition tool (e.g. Bonusly) + a rewards budget = $X
  - Crewhu Recognize or Grow = $Y
  - *Get the client to sign off the numbers. Don't publish this unless Crewhu wins, or reframe it as cost per capability.*
- Note Crewhu's 60-day money-back guarantee on annual plans and its implementation fee. Don't hide the fee.

**7. H2 – MSPs who switched from SmileBack to Crewhu**
- Embedded videos: **Platinum Systems** (+17% survey conversion in 10 months) and **InnoTek** (higher CSAT and better culture in 6 months), each with `VideoObject` schema and a transcript excerpt.
- 2–3 Capterra quotes that mention SmileBack, linked to the review.

**8. H2 – How to switch from SmileBack to Crewhu**
- 4–5 numbered steps: connect PSA → import survey history (Grow plan "Survey Import") → map technicians → choose badges and metrics → go live. Give a realistic timeline.
- This targets bottom-of-funnel searches ("migrate from smileback") and supports the secondary CTA.

**9. H2 – Verdict: which should you choose?**
- **Choose SmileBack if:** … (2–3 bullets)
- **Choose Crewhu if:** … (3–4 bullets)
- One closing sentence that repeats the answer box in different words, since AIO often quotes verdict sections.

**10. H2 – FAQ** (build from People Also Ask and AIO follow-ups; 2–4 sentences each)
- Is SmileBack the same as ConnectWise Customer Feedback?
- Who owns SmileBack?
- Is Crewhu better than SmileBack?
- How much does SmileBack cost compared with Crewhu?
- Does Crewhu work with Autotask / HaloPSA / Kaseya BMS?
- Can I use SmileBack and Crewhu together?
- Can I import my SmileBack survey history into Crewhu?
- Does SmileBack have employee recognition or gamification?
- What's the best SmileBack alternative for MSPs?

**11. Sources & methodology** (small print)
List every external figure with its link and check date: Capterra, G2, ConnectWise pricing, the acquisition press release. Add one line: "We're Crewhu, so we're biased. Here's how we kept this fair."

**12. Final CTA band:** demo + migration call + "See all comparisons" (link to the `/compare/` hub).

- **Out from this page:** `/collect-customer-feedback`, `/recognize-your-team`, `/gamify-your-metrics`, `/improve-your-online-rep`, `/pricing`, `/integrations/connectwise` (once built), the GO2 Tech and XL.net case studies, `/blog/what-is-csat`.
- **In to this page:** `/video-library` (next to the switcher videos), `/blog/customer-feedback-software`, `/blog/employee-recognition-software`, `/pricing` FAQ, the homepage footer "Compare" menu.

### Content rules for every vs page
- Date the tables and recheck them every quarter; stale prices undermine the whole page.
- Back every claim about the competitor with a link to its own page or a third-party source.
- Don't say anything negative that isn't sourced. Use "not offered" rather than "lacks".
- Use the competitor's brand name in headings and copy (normal comparative use), but no logos unless legal approves.

## 4. How the outline changes for other opponents

### Crewhu vs. Simplesat *(Archetype A: like-for-like)*
- **Frame:** same job, different depth. Lead with the **feature table** (it's the main part of the page here, unlike SmileBack).
- **Add:** survey types (Simplesat offers CES too, so acknowledge it), multi-question logic, branding, response rates, support (G2 rates Simplesat highly for support), pricing tier by tier ($119 Standard → $499 Elite, to be verified).
- **Crewhu's angle:** recognition and gamification are still the gap, but Simplesat is broader than SmileBack on surveys, so put the emphasis on **MSP-specific** depth (technician attribution, KPI contests).
- **Leave out:** the ownership/independence section (Simplesat is independent).

### Crewhu vs. built-in PSA surveys *(Archetype D: native / do nothing)*
- **H1 idea:** "ConnectWise Built-In Surveys vs. Crewhu: When Is Native CSAT Enough?"
- **Main part:** a **capability ladder** instead of a vendor table: Native (email link, basic score) → Dedicated CSAT tool → Feedback-to-recognition platform. Mark where each rung stops (e.g. one-click response, conditional logic, reporting history, technician leaderboards).
- **Add:** a response-rate argument (Mirus IT got 2.5% returns before Crewhu); a **"native is enough if…"** section; an ROI calculator CTA.
- **Leave out:** a competitor-brand verdict or "switching" stories (nobody "switches" from free). Use "upgrading" language.
- One page per PSA (ConnectWise, Autotask, Halo), because the native limits differ and searches are PSA-specific.

### Crewhu vs. HappySat *(Archetype A, with a price-led opponent)*
- HappySat starts at about $49/mo and positions itself against SimpleSat, SmileBack **and CrewHu**. Expect buyers to arrive comparing on price.
- **Lead with:** value per dollar and what you get beyond surveys. Include a "HappySat is a fit if budget is the only factor" section.
- **Add:** company maturity and track record (customer count, case studies, years in market) as a trust table. HappySat has much less third-party coverage.
- **Thin-data rule:** if a competitor has few public sources, keep the page shorter and stick to facts. Don't pad it with guesses.

### Crewhu vs. an agency or service *(Archetype F)*
- **No feature table.** Use a **responsibilities table**: who designs surveys, who chases responses, who runs recognition, who reports, who owns the data, with rows for In-house + Crewhu / Agency / Hybrid.
- **Add:** a 12-month cost comparison (retainer vs. subscription + internal hours), time to first result, what happens when the contract ends, a "use both" option (the agency runs the program on Crewhu).
- **CTA:** "Talk about running it in-house" or a partner-program link, not a hard demo push.
