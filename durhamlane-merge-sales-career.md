## 2. What to move into the kept page

The two posts make the same six or seven points, often in the same words. So the merge is mostly about **framing and a few unique paragraphs**, plus new sections for the intent that neither page covers well.

### A. Take these from the merge page

| # | Move | Where it goes on the kept page | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The question framing.** Make the title and H1 ask "Is sales a good career?" | Title tag, H1, first sentence | This is why the merge page ranks at 11.1 for the head query. See the proposed title in section 3. |
| 2 | **The more recent stat:** "an estimated **8%** of the UK workforce currently works in sales and sales-related professions". Name the source (ONS Labour Force Survey) and the year. | Intro, replacing the older "7%, some 2.2 million" line | The kept page uses an older figure. Both openings with stats are near-duplicates, so keep one current, sourced figure. |
| 3 | **The multilingual / cultural diversity paragraph** ("…sales is also perhaps the most culturally diverse profession there is… multilingual salespeople are always highly sought after") | End of "2. Sales is diverse" | Unique to the merge page. It's also durhamlane's own differentiator (multilingual SDR teams). |
| 4 | **Career paths sentence** ("From inside sales, to sales operations and business development… many career paths and many levels") | "4. Sales is for life", expanded into a short career-ladder list: SDR/BDR → Account Executive → Team Lead/Sales Manager → Head of Sales; plus Sales Ops/RevOps, Customer Success and Account Management | Covers "is sales a good career path" (pos 7.1 on the merge page), "career in sales" and "pharma sales to commercial management career path" |
| 5 | **"…can be learned and improved through sales training"** and "skills you can apply in many other areas in life" | "3. Sales gives you life skills". Link to https://durhamlane.com/blog/sales-development-training/ | Unique wording. Adds an internal link. |
| 6 | **The "There aren't many careers that offer the chance to be instantly rewarded when you perform well" line** | "6. Sales is rewarding" | Stronger than the kept page's copy |
| 7 | **The closing careers CTA** ("Still interested in a sales career? View durhamlane careers…") | End of the post, as a styled CTA box linking to https://durhamlane.com/about/careers/ | The kept page has no careers CTA, and this is a recruitment page. |

Nothing else on the merge page is unique. Its points 1–6 repeat the kept page's points 1–7, with 5 and 6 combined. **Don't copy those sections across.**

## 4. Merge checklist

1. Publish the updated kept page (sections 2 and 3).
2. **301** `https://durhamlane.com/blog/is-sales-a-good-career-choice/` → `https://durhamlane.com/blog/7-reasons-why-sales-is-a-great-career/`. Also redirect the no-slash version `/blog/is-sales-a-good-career-choice`, which already redirects to the slash version. Make it one hop.
3. Remove the merged post from the sitemap (Yoast does this automatically once it's a redirect or trashed), and update any internal links to it.
4. Also 301 the old 404 variants that still get impressions to the kept page: `/blog/7-reason-why-sales-is-a-great-career`, `/blog/7-reasons-why-sales-a-great-career/`, `/blog/7-reasons-why-sales-jest-a-great-career`.
5. In GSC, use URL Inspection → Request indexing on the kept page.
6. **Measure after 4–6 weeks.** Compare the kept page for "is sales a good career" (target: position ≤ 10, currently 17.9). Check the combined clicks for both URLs against the 12-month baseline (137 clicks, 44.7K impressions).
