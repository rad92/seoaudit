UpKeep Sprint 1: what we found and what we need

Meeting 7 Oct 2026Attendees Stuart (UpKeep), Alex, Radi (Slingshot)Length 30 min

Google and AI search engines can't tell clearly what UpKeep is, which pages to rank, or why to trust it. Over the next two weeks, UpKeep fixes the brand facts and the site errors, and Slingshot rebuilds the content.

1

What we found

Six problems, starting with the one that costs the most.

Top priority
5 brand facts that conflict

UpKeep describes itself differently in different places

Category (CMMS, EAM or asset operations), tagline, product names, founding year, pricing and customer count all vary across About Us, PR, G2 and Capterra. When the facts conflict, AI tools cite UpKeep less.

High
0 industry case studies with numbers

Not enough proof

There are no case studies with named customers and real results, and reviews don't mention industry-specific work. Google and AI tools look for this kind of evidence.

High
12 broken links (404s) in the /es nav and footer

Spanish site serves English content and broken links

The /es homepage is mostly in English, which Google treats as duplicate content. Product links skip the /es pages. These pages earn about 14,350 clicks, so they're worth fixing rather than removing.

Template
3 site-wide template issues

Template problems on key pages

On product pages, the nav headings load before the H1. The footer address doesn't link to UpKeep's Google Business Profile. The homepage has no security or award badges, which MaintainX shows.

Content
~260 pages to merge or redirect

Pages compete with each other

We reviewed 744 blog and learning URLs in 180 topic groups. Many pages cover the same topic, so they compete with each other. For example, "best CMMS software" gets 68,427 impressions but only 0.2% CTR, and a second page competes with it.

Content
34 clicks from ~17k impressions

Comparison pages don't convert search traffic

There are 10 "UpKeep vs" pages covering MaintainX, Fiix, eMaint, Limble, Coast and EZO. Blog and product versions split the traffic, and the content needs accurate input from your team.

Details for each item

Why it matters, what to do, and what to send us.

1Brand facts▸
Why it matters

AI answers draw on G2, Capterra, press releases and your own site. When these sources disagree about what UpKeep is, AI tools trust UpKeep less and cite it less often.

Decide
  • Category: CMMS, EAM, asset operations platform or maintenance platform
  • Modifier: AI-native, mobile-first, or AI built in
  • Value points: for example, less downtime, % of time saved on reports, built for technicians
  • Product names: one name for each product, used everywhere
  • Key stats: founding year (About Us and press releases disagree), pricing plans, current number of customers
Then update, starting with

Capterra, G2, About Us, homepage, /product

Send us

A one-page brand facts sheet with the final category, modifier, value points, product names and stats.

2Industry case studies▸
Why it matters

Concrete results are the evidence Google and AI tools quote. A quote alone isn't enough. A named person and company with a link makes it credible.

What good looks like

"Company A (food and beverage) cut maintenance spend by 44% in Q1 after rolling out UpKeep." The spokesperson is linked to their LinkedIn or company page.

Where to start

Any industry. Our highest-traffic industry pages are facility management, restaurants, property management, hospitality and fitness.

Send us

Ideally one case study per industry, with real numbers and linked names. Short customer interviews would also help a lot.

3Industry-specific reviews▸
Why it matters

Generic reviews such as "great tool" don't help. Reviews that use industry terms show which industries UpKeep serves.

Terms customers might use (food and beverage example)

FDA preventive controls, GFSI, BRCGS, FSSC 22000, GMP, CIP (clean-in-place), allergen changeovers, calibration of metal detectors, scales and thermometers

How
  • Ask customers to describe how they actually use UpKeep in their industry. Don't write the review for them.
  • Send some reviewers to the Google Business Profile as well as G2 and Capterra.
Send us

A note when new reviews go live, so we can check them.

4Comparison-page framework▸
Why it matters

Comparison searches come from buyers who are close to deciding. We're rebuilding all 10 pages, and the claims about features and pricing have to be accurate.

Pages

MaintainX (product + blog) · Fiix (product + blog) · eMaint (product + blog) · Limble (product + blog) · Coast · EZO

How
  • Open the framework file in a browser and fill in every field.
  • Click Export, choose HTML document, then download.html, and send us the file.
Send us

The completed framework as an HTML file.

5Footer address link▸
Problem

The footer address (10880 Wilshire Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90024) links to a generic Google Maps address instead of UpKeep's business listing.

Fix

Replace the link with the Google Maps link to UpKeep's Google Business Profile. This helps Google connect the listing with the brand.

Send us

A quick message once it's live.

6Nav headings before the H1▸
Problem

On every product page, the menu renders headings before the H1, so search engines see the menu as the start of the page. Some pages also put H4s directly after H2s.

Fix

Stop rendering the menu labels as heading tags (H2 to H4) in the template, and clean up the heading order. The same problem appears on the /es pages.

Send us

A confirmation once it's deployed.

8Homepage trust bar▸
Why it matters

MaintainX's homepage shows awards and security badges, which build trust with buyers and AI tools. UpKeep's homepage doesn't show either.

What to add
  • Security certifications and award badges under "Trusted by 4,000+ companies"
  • Optional: link each customer logo to a page that shows the company uses UpKeep
Send us

A design or staging link for us to review before it goes live.

9Redirects for finished page groups▸
Why it matters

Merging duplicate pages makes the remaining page stronger and removes the competition between them. The 301 redirects keep the existing links and traffic.

What your developer does
  • Set up the 301 redirects from our plan for the groups we've finished
  • Update every internal link that points to a redirected URL
  • Apply the same changes to the /es versions
Send us

A note when each batch is live. We'll check for redirect chains and 404s.
