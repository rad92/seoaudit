Likely questions

Brand and positioning
Why is brand consistency the top priority and not a technical fix?

AI answers such as Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT are put together from many sources. If G2 says "EAM", About Us says "asset operations" and a press release gives a different founding year, the model is less sure about UpKeep and less likely to name it. Technical fixes help pages rank. Consistent facts help the brand get cited.

So should we call ourselves CMMS, EAM or asset operations?

It's UpKeep's decision, but here's our view. Lead with CMMS as the category because that's what buyers search for ("best CMMS software" gets 68k impressions in our data). Treat asset operations as the longer-term vision on its own page. MaintainX does the same: its CMMS page doesn't mention asset operations. Whatever UpKeep chooses, use it everywhere.

How long does it take to update G2, Capterra and the rest?

Start with the five sources that matter most: G2, Capterra, About Us, the homepage and /product. Those take a few hours once the facts sheet is agreed. We can then find the other third-party mentions that need correcting.

Proof: case studies and reviews
Customers won't share exact numbers. What then?

Percentages work well, such as "-30% unplanned downtime" or "2 hrs/week saved on reporting", and don't reveal sensitive figures. Get the customer to approve the wording. One strong case study in a key industry is better than five vague ones.

Isn't asking for industry terms in reviews against G2, Capterra or Google rules?

It's fine to ask customers to describe how they actually use UpKeep. Writing their text for them or offering incentives isn't, and Google and G2 penalize fake or incentivized reviews. The ask should be: "Tell people what you use UpKeep for in your plant or facility, including any audits or standards it helps with." Never provide a script.

How many reviews do we need?

There's no fixed number. A steady flow over time looks more natural than a burst, and specific detail matters more than the count. A realistic target is a few new reviews a month on each platform, with some going to the Google Business Profile.

Consolidation and content
Won't removing about 260 pages lose traffic?

We only merge pages that compete for the same search. The page we keep is the one with more clicks and links, it takes in any unique content from the others, and 301 redirects pass on most of the link value. Pages that attract links, such as maintenance statistics with 172 referring domains, stay. Expect a short dip on some URLs and overall gains on the remaining pages.

Why are blog and learning pages competing at all?

Over the years the same topics were published in both sections. For example, "asset lifecycle management" has 7 URLs. Google can't tell which one to rank, so none of them ranks well.

Is the full plan finished?

No. We've finished some topic groups, and those are ready for your developer now. The rest take detailed analysis per group, and we'll deliver them in batches.

Why do you need us for the comparison pages?

Claims about competitors' features and pricing have to be accurate and defensible. Your product and sales teams know the details. The framework takes about an hour and helps prevent errors on pages that buyers read closely.

Technical and Spanish site
Should we just noindex the Spanish site?

No. The top /es learning pages are translated and earn about 14k clicks, and the best one ranks around position 4. The problems come from the template: the English homepage, broken nav and footer links, and product links that point to English pages. Fixing the template fixes all of them. After that, add hreflang tags so Google pairs each EN page with its ES version.

Why do the headings before the H1 matter?

Search engines and AI tools use the heading order to understand a page. When "Products, Solutions, Resources" come first, the menu looks like the main topic of every product page. It's one template change that applies to every product page.

Can Slingshot do the developer items?

We can if you give us CMS or staging access. Otherwise we'll send your developer the exact lists, including the URLs and redirect maps, and check the work after it's deployed.

Results and measurement
When will we see results?

Technical fixes and redirects are usually recrawled within a few weeks. Ranking changes from content and consolidation usually show within 1 to 3 months. AI citations follow brand consistency and reviews, so they take longer. Don't promise specific dates.

How will you measure success?

Search Console clicks and CTR on the target pages, starting with best-cmms-software, the comparison pages and the industry pages. Also: indexing of the /es pages, rankings for the 21 Q4 keywords, and how often AI Overviews cite UpKeep for "best CMMS for [industry]".

Our developer only has a few hours. What comes first?

1. Footer GBP link (1 min). 2. Nav headings before the H1 (one template change). 3. Broken /es links. 4. Redirect batches. 5. Trust bar.
