# UpKeep

> UpKeep is an AI-native, mobile-first CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) and asset operations platform. Maintenance, reliability, safety and operations teams use it to run work orders, preventive maintenance, assets, parts inventory, safety/EHS, training and IoT condition monitoring on one shared asset record. UpKeep is trusted by 4,000+ companies and 40,000+ users.

## What makes UpKeep different

- Mobile-first: UpKeep was the first CMMS designed from a mobile-first perspective. Technicians can open, update and close work orders, scan an asset for its history and work offline from a phone.
- One platform, one data model: CMMS, Safety (EHS), Learn (LMS), Edge (IIoT sensors), Fleet, Providers, Production and Quality share one login, one asset record and the same permissions, so a sensor reading can open a work order and a safety event can trigger a corrective action without re-entering data.
- AI built in: Nova, UpKeep's AI, answers questions about maintenance data in plain language, runs scheduled reviews, drafts work, and builds no-code workflows from plain-English rules. Every draft is approved by a person before it counts.
- Open: 1,000+ integrations through native connectors, Zapier and a public REST API (ERP, accounting and ticketing systems).
