H2

The core difference: measuring satisfaction vs. acting on it

Writer notes

Opening line
"SmileBack tells you how a client felt about a ticket. Crewhu sends that answer to the technician, the leaderboard and the rewards store."
Cover
  • Both send one-click surveys when a ticket closes. Say this plainly.
  • With SmileBack, the score goes to dashboards and alerts.
  • With Crewhu, the same score triggers recognition, points and review requests on its own.
  • Why it matters: technicians see the feedback, so engagement and retention improve.
Proof point
Pick one: XL.net +20% engagement and 95% CSAT, or Succurri's quote "make informed decisions based on measured facts."

When SmileBack is the better choice

Bullet list (3–4 items), each with a short explanation

Writer notes

Bullets
  • You only need CSAT/NPS and don't plan to run a recognition program.
  • A very small team on a tight budget: per-agent pricing is cheaper for surveys alone.
  • You're fully on ConnectWise and want fewer vendors.

Guideline

Why
