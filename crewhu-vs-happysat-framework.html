A5 · GapsRanked by impact

Gaps in the current results that Crewhu can take

HighNo comparison exists

HappySat names Crewhu as replaceable but never explains how. The AI says "no direct comparison found".

Use: publish first and become the default source.

HighNo third-party trust signals for HappySat

No G2 or Capterra ratings, no case studies, no response-rate data. Crewhu has 190+ directory reviews and named MSP results.

Use: a factual "track record" table. Show what's published for each and let readers judge.

High"Replace" without listing what's lost

HappySat replaces Crewhu's surveys. Recognition, contests, rewards and Echo have no counterpart in any source.

Use: an "If you switch, you keep / you lose" table in the switching section.

HighCrewhu Echo is invisible in comparisons

Echo imports Google reviews, links them to technicians and clients, drafts AI replies, and powers review widgets. No comparison page mentions it, including Crewhu's own planned pages.

Use: give Echo its own H3 here, and add it to the SmileBack and Simplesat pages.

MediumWrong Crewhu prices in directories

"$99 per user" (Capterra) and "$4 per user" (ITQlick) both circulate.

Use: dated pricing table, plus correcting the listings.

MediumPrice questions with no clear answer

HappySat's tiers ($49 → $499) are only on a bundle page, and what counts as a "user" is unclear.

Use: a clear "what each price includes" table, confirmed with both vendors' public pages.

VerifyPSA write-back depth

HappySat lists the same PSAs (plus RepairShopr) but no source describes write-back or technician mapping.

Use: only a table row, written as "not documented" until confirmed.

A6 · Honest strengthsBoth columns go on the page in some form

Where each product wins

Crewhu's advantages

  • Feedback triggers recognition, points, contests and rewards. HappySat lists none of these.
  • Crewhu Echo: Google reviews monitored, matched to technicians, answered with AI drafts.
  • 190+ verified reviews (Capterra 4.9, G2 4.7) vs none found for HappySat.
  • Six published MSP case studies plus video testimonials.
  • One product, one roadmap: the whole company works on this platform.
  • Implementation consultant on every plan; 60-day money-back on annual.

HappySat's advantages (concede these)

  • Much cheaper entry ($49/mo) for CSAT and NPS.
  • Bundled with five other MSP tools (client portal, warranty, backup alerts, time, vCIO) in Stack Saver.
  • Built by an MSP owner; MSP-only focus.
  • RepairShopr integration (break-fix shops).
  • Simple 4-smiley survey; quick to roll out.
A7 · StrategyHow this page differs from SmileBack and Simplesat

Page strategy

  • Archetype: like-for-like with a price-led opponent that has little public data. Per the framework: shorter page, lead with value per dollar, add a track-record table, no guesses.
  • Main frame: "Budget CSAT survey tool vs. a feedback-to-recognition platform with a track record." Both are MSP-specific, so "built for MSPs" can't be the differentiator here.
  • Main battlegrounds: (1) what each price buys, (2) published proof and reviews, (3) what happens after the survey, including Echo.
  • New sections vs other pages: Track record (Block 06). No PSA-matrix section and no response-rate section: HappySat publishes no data to compare against.
  • Tone: extra care. Invarosoft is small and founder-led; anything that reads as punching down will backfire with MSP buyers who know both founders. Facts and "not listed" only.
  • Length: 1,300–1,800 words. Low search volume does not justify a 2,500-word page.
  • Cross-page action: add a Crewhu Echo H3 and table row to the SmileBack and Simplesat frameworks.
Part B · Page wireframe
Be fair, cite everything

Every claim about HappySat links to invarosoft.com or a third-party source. Write "not listed", never "lacks" or "doesn't have".

Date the facts

HappySat's prices come from a bundle page that may be out of date. Show "Last verified: [date]" and recheck every quarter.

Write for quoting

Open each H2 with a 1–2 sentence direct answer. AI Overviews lift these lines.

Voice

Plain, respectful, MSP-insider. No "BADASS", no jokes at a small vendor's expense.

Length

1,300–1,800 words in total. Tables do most of the work.

Names

Write "HappySat" and "Invarosoft" as the company does. No competitor logos without legal sign-off.
